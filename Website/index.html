Entropy in the Stock Market

A research project that uses Shannon entropy to study whether Indian IT stocks enter unusually ordered periods at the same time and how long they take to return to normal.

Key Result

Two-stage recovery 6.2 days to exit the low-entropy state; 25.7 days to return to normal

Stocks fall into ordered states together, but each recovers on its own clock.

Nifty 50 Volatility Analysis

A web dashboard that shows how often the Nifty 50 makes large daily moves and whether those moves occur more frequently near options expiry.

Key Result

Large-move frequency 8.3% of trading days moved by at least 2%

Expiry-week risk isn't more frequent, but it does skew more bearish.

Factor Regression and Hedge Research

A CAPM/factor-regression study on three NSE stocks against Nifty 50 and their sector indices, used to test whether a beta-sized Nifty futures position works as a hedge.

Key Result

Sector, not market, drives it Market beta is statistically zero for 2 of 3 stocks once sector is controlled for

A full hedge cancels gains and losses together. It's a risk tool, not a strategy.
